Ask WalkDots.

What makes WalkDots different from an audio guide?

Nothing is pre-recorded. WalkDots builds the tour around wherever you’re standing, narrates it as you move, and answers whatever you ask: who built it, what happened here, whether it’s worth going inside.

Type where you are and the tour is built there. Pick what you care about, and WalkDots strings together the stops worth your feet.

The guide talks as you walk, so keep the phone in your pocket if you like. Each story starts when you reach the stop.

Ask about what you’re looking at — who built it, what happened here, whether it’s worth going inside — and get an answer before the next corner.

Spoken, turn-by-turn directions to the next stop. Missed it? Say “repeat directions” and it tells you again.

Short on time? Get the three things worth knowing about a stop in under a minute, then keep moving.
