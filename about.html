About

The guide in your ear, for any city.

WalkDots plans a walk around what you actually want to see, then tells you the story of every stop as you reach it.

What WalkDots is

WalkDots is an audio guide for walking a city on your own. Tell it how long you have and what you are curious about — history, food, hidden corners, music, legends — and it plans a walk from where you are standing, with real walking directions between the stops.

At every stop a guide tells you the story of the place in your ear, written for the walk you asked for rather than read from a guidebook. When something catches your eye, ask about it, and the guide answers.

How it works

  • Built around you. The length of the walk, how much detail, your pace and your interests decide which stops you get and what you hear at each one.
  • Anywhere. WalkDots plans a tour in any city, from your location or a city you choose.
  • In your language. The tour is written and spoken in the language you pick.
  • On the web and on iPhone. Open it in your browser at app.walkdots.com. An account is optional; signing in keeps your walks on all your devices.
